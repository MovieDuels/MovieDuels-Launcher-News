We have reuploaded Update 8 to ModDB and Nexus Mods with several bug fixes and small improvements.

List of changes:

  • Updated the launcher to detect old files that were overriding Update 8 files.
  • Fixed JKA combat mode requiring the player to hold the attack button longer than a single press.
  • Fixed dual-saber NPCs from switching to fast stance when facing enemies using a staff or dual sabers.
  • Fixed saber attacks dealing less damage than intended.
  • Fixed blaster NPCs targeting the player when "notarget" is enabled.
  • Fixed Luke's saber walk animation in JKA and MD mode.
  • Fixed saber damage when trying to escape from a wampa.
  • Fixed Beskar armour carrying over when changing characters.
  • Fixed the jetpack consuming fuel too quickly.
  • Fixed an issue that prevented Jedi NPCs from attacking at range.
  • Fixed force lightning crashing the game when using Rend2.
  • Added the ability to toggle snowtrooper follow behaviour at the start of the Battle of Hoth mission.
  • Added invisible walls around the rooftop in The Wrong Jedi mission.
  • Enabled air dash and double jump for all combat modes.
  • Increased saber stats for powerful characters to make them more challenging to fight.
  • Reduced the time it takes for blaster NPCs to get back up after being knocked down.
  • Removed the idle blaster animation that played while aiming.
  • Added improved squad tactics for blaster NPCs when NPC difficulty is set to Hard.
  • Added quick keys for God Mode, No Target, and No Clip to the controls menu.
  • Re-added the Jedi Outcast HUD option.

The Jedi Outcast addon has been updated to fix Kyle not having level 3 force jump in the final few Yavin levels.

Please download the mod again from ModDB or Nexus Mods to get the latest version.
